The National Curriculum Council (NCC) is a federal body created in 2014 to develop national curriculum standards. NCC achieved a major milestone on April 13, 2023, where for the first time in Pakistan's history, a core curriculum from Grades 1-12 and early years was signed off by subject experts from all provinces. 

I served as one of the subject matter experts and drafted a section of Pakistan's CS national curriculum for grades 9-12 related to the domains of computer systems and the impacts of computing. This work involved collaborating with a large team of CS experts to update and create the current CS standards.

This work has impacted 14 million secondary school students, the 7th largest in the world. I was honored to receive a certificate of appreciation and a shield from the National Curriculum Council of Pakistan in recognition of this work.
