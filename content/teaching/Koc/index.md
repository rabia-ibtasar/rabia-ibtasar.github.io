During a recent trip to Istanbul, I had the pleasure of joining a graduate class at Koç Üniversitesi as a guest speaker, at the invitation of my friend and colleague, Dr. Bengisu Çağıltay. I shared my work on tangible designs for museum exhibits which opened a conversation about designs of social robots.

<!--- ![With Dr. Bengisu Çağıltay](me_bengisu.JPG) --->

Together, we explored questions of trust and agency in designing interactions for social robots in modern society. The students brought thoughtful insights and questions to the discussion and I left with new ideas to consider. I’m grateful to Dr. Çağıltay and her students for such an engaging experience.
