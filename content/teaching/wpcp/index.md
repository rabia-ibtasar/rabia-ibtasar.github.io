I was invited to speak with Girls Who Code at Walter Payton College Prep, where I shared my personal and professional experiences as a woman in computer science. The students asked thoughtful questions about stereotypes in computing and discussed ways to challenge them. We also explored social robotics through demonstrations and an interactive activity in which students brainstormed designs for their own social robots.
