Through my work with Breakthrough Tech Chicago, I have supported efforts to broaden participation in computer science as a coach and course co-instructor.

## Teaching & Coaching

As a co-instructor, I supported students’ engagement with computing concepts and activities. As a coach, I provided guidance and encouragement as students navigated their learning experiences.

This work connects with my broader research interests in belonging, interest development, and the social supports that help students participate in computing.
