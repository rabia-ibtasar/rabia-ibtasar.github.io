I have supported efforts to broaden participation in computer science in our local community as a Breakthrough Tech Chicago coach, mentor and course co-instructor.

I served as a Sprinternship Coach with Break Through Tech in April–May 2023 and April 2024, mentoring undergraduate women in computer science during industry-sponsored projects. I guided students in planning their work, developing professional skills, and navigating the expectations and experiences of a workplace setting.

My coaching focused on helping students approach unfamiliar challenges with confidence and reflect on what they were learning. I supported them as they considered how to contribute to a project, communicate with others, and connect their computing education to professional opportunities.

## Teaching & Coaching

As a Graduate Teaching Assistant and Instructor for CS 111: Introduction to Programming at the University of Illinois Chicago, I supported students learning foundational programming concepts. My responsibilities included instruction, grading, and mentoring students as they developed their computing skills.

This work connects with my broader research interests in belonging, interest development, and the social supports that help students participate in computing and increase underrepresented students' retention in computing in the long-term.
