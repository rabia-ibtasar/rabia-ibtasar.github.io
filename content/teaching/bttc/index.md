As a mentor and coach for Breakthrough Tech Chicago, I have supported efforts to broaden participation in computer science in our local community. as a coach and course co-instructor.

## Teaching & Coaching

As a co-instructor for CS 1 computing concepts and activities. As a coach, I provided guidance and encouragement as students navigated their learning experiences.

This work connects with my broader research interests in belonging, interest development, and the social supports that help students participate in computing.
