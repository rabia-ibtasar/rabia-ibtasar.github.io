STEMMates investigates how a social robot can support middle school students’ science reading and learning at home.

Built on the Misty robot platform, the STEMMate learning companion provides comments during reading activities designed to support comprehension, social connection, and interest in science.

The project brings together learning sciences and human–robot interaction to explore how interactions with a robot can complement educational materials and support learning beyond the classroom.

[Learn more on the lab website](https://www.uiclitlab.com/stemmates)
